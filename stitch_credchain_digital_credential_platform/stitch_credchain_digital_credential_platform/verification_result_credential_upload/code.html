Verification & Ledger FAQs

How does on-chain cryptographic verification work? expand_more
CredChain computes a deterministic SHA-256 hash of the credential payload and checks it against the Merkle tree root anchored on our public decentralized ledger. The signature is mathematically guaranteed to be issued by Global Tech Institute's published DID.
Why is the Milestone Trail locked by default? expand_more
To comply with modern sovereign privacy guidelines (GDPR & FERPA), intermediate milestones and grades remain encrypted under the credential holder's private key. Viewers can request temporary zero-knowledge access from the student or employer.
Can this verification be presented offline or printed? expand_more
Yes. You can use your browser's Print feature or the print icon at the top of this page to export an official PDF transcript with the cryptographic verification footer intact.
