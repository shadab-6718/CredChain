policy Revocation Protocol

Under W3C Decentralized Identifier and Verifiable Credentials (VC) specifications, revocations are published into an append-only cryptographic accumulator.

  • Zero-knowledge proof verify status instantaneously.
  • Wallet holders are dispatched a webhook notification upon ledger inclusion.
  • Records cannot be erased; state is changed to REVOKED.
